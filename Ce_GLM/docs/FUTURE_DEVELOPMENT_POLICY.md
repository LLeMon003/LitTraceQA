# Future Development Policy

Version 2.3 is the formal immutable baseline for future LitTraceQA reproduction work.

Future optimization should begin from a Git branch or an unchanged versioned copy of this baseline, not from the research archive. Recommended branch names include:

- `experiment/<name>`
- `feature/<name>`
- `release/<future-version>`

Historical Version 2.3 metrics and manifests must not be overwritten. Experimental stronger-model profiles must remain separate from the historical fresh-compatibility profile, and new checkpoints require new hashes, manifests, and version numbers.

The complete research archive is separate from this repository and is not uploaded here.
